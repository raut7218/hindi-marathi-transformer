Figures
=======

This folder collects links to the training and evaluation plots produced by the experiments.

The PNGs are stored in `checkpoints_colab/plots/` and are referenced here for convenience:

- Loss curve: checkpoints_colab/plots/loss.png
- BLEU-100 curve: checkpoints_colab/plots/bleu_100.png
- CHRF++-100 curve: checkpoints_colab/plots/chrfpp_100.png

Open the images in your viewer or click the links in the repository browser to inspect them.
